THEME (applies to every image on the site):
- Motif: trees and plants. Branches, leaves, roots, seeds, saplings, rings of a trunk, bark, vines.
- Look: clean 3D computer graphics. Sharp, crisp, nicely rendered, simple. A modern product-style render: smooth matte materials, soft studio lighting from the upper left, gentle contact shadows, subtle ambient occlusion. Not faceted low-poly, not pixel art, not hand-painted, not photoreal, not clip-art flat vector.
- Composition: one clear subject, few elements, generous negative space. Readable at 320 px wide.
- Ground: every image sits on a plain, solid, deep forest green background, exactly #0f2418, edge to edge. No scenery, sky, horizon, floor plane or vignette; the only thing on the ground is a soft contact shadow.
- Palette for the forms: leaf green #00a24c, bright mint #67e387, warm wood browns, cream #f4efe6 as a light material. A small amber #f0b756 accent is allowed. Forms must stand out clearly against the dark ground: no dark or deep-green main shapes, no black.
- Forbidden: text, letters, numbers, logos (unless the brief says so), people, animals, mascots, faces, hands.
