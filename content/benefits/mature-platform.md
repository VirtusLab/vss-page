title: Mature platform
tagline: "Scala leverages the most mature runtime available."
---

Scala runs on the JVM, a platform with decades of innovation, research 
and hardening. Java's Garbage Collector has been fine-tuned so to meet the
requirements of web-scale and enterprise services.

Scala seamlessly interoperates with Java, opening the door to a vast ecosystem
of libraries, so that most problems can be solved by reusing code, instead of
writing it anew.
